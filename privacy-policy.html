Your privacy, clearly stated.

We keep this simple. Here is exactly what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. No legal jargon designed to confuse.

Last updated: May 2026

Who We Are

Bridgewell Advisory is a specialist recruitment advisory and HR business consulting firm based in Bengaluru, India. We operate at bridgewell-advisory.in. References to "we", "us" or "Bridgewell" in this policy refer to Bridgewell Advisory.

What Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you:

  • Submit a consultation request or enquiry form
  • Contact us via email, phone or WhatsApp
  • Engage with us on LinkedIn or other platforms
  • Participate in a hiring or HR advisory engagement

This may include your name, company name, email address, phone number, job title, and details about your hiring or HR requirements. We do not collect sensitive personal data unless directly relevant to the engagement.

How We Use Your Information

We use information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or consultation request
  • Deliver recruitment advisory or HR consulting services you have engaged us for
  • Communicate relevant updates about your engagement
  • Improve how we work and the services we provide

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing. We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Candidate Information

If you are a candidate introduced to a client through Bridgewell, we handle your information with the same care. Your profile is shared only with clients relevant to your search, with your knowledge. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by writing to support@bridgewell-advisory.in.

Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to deliver the service or as required by applicable law. For active engagement records, we retain data for up to 3 years post-engagement unless you request earlier deletion. Enquiry-only contacts where no engagement follows are retained for 12 months.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@bridgewell-advisory.in.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website uses cookies and basic analytics to understand how visitors use the site. See our Cookie Policy for full details. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services which may process data on our behalf:

  • Tally.so — for consultation booking forms
  • Google Analytics — for anonymised website analytics
  • WhatsApp — for direct communication where you initiate contact

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Our team accesses client information only on a need-to-know basis for the purpose of delivering your engagement.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted with a revised date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website or services after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Questions about this policy?

If you have any questions, concerns or requests regarding this policy or how we handle your information, reach out to us directly. We will respond within 5 business days.
