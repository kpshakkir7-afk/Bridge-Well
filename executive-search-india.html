Why Leadership Search Fails

The failure is decided before the search starts.

01
The role is a wish list
Ten priorities, no first principle. Nobody can be excellent at all of them, so the panel settles.
02
The market was never mapped
Reachable names get called. The leader who would actually move was never approached.
03
Speed beats fit
A board wants the seat filled. The hire is comfortable, not right, and it shows in eighteen months.
