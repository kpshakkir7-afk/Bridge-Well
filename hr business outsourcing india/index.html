05 How We Fix It

Three steps. In this exact order.

Every Bridgewell engagement runs in the same sequence. Order matters. Most foundation work fails because it skips a step or gets the order wrong.

— 01
People

Define who does what, who decides what, and who reports to whom.

Org structure, levelling, role charters, decision rights, and reporting clarity. The people layer is built first because process and performance depend on it.

  • Org design & reporting structure
  • Levelling framework (titles, bands, span)
  • Role charters & decision rights
  • Talent acquisition architecture
  • Total rewards design (comp, ESOP, benefits)
  • Employer Value Proposition build
— 02
Process

Write down how the work actually gets done — for every important task.

Documented standard operating procedures across hiring, onboarding, performance, comp, exits, compliance, and culture. The process layer turns clarity into consistency.

  • Hiring SOPs & interview discipline
  • Onboarding system (day-one to ninety)
  • HR tech stack & people operations
  • Policy, POSH, and compliance setup
  • Exit and offboarding workflows
  • Culture rituals & operating cadence
— 03
Performance

Set up tracking and weekly reviews so the business corrects itself.

Goal-setting, KPIs, calibration, ratings, and review cadences. The performance layer turns consistency into compounding outcomes.

  • OKR or KPI framework
  • Quarterly review & calibration cycle
  • People dashboard & analytics
  • Performance enablement system
  • Manager training tracks
  • Succession & capability mapping
Order matters. Writing down processes before defining roles doesn't work. Tracking performance before defining processes is just noise.
