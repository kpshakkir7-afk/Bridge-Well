Advisory vs Volume

We send fewer candidates.
Better decisions.

Most agencies move fast and send many. We move deliberately and send few, with a clear recommendation on each. The difference is accountability.

Advisory (Bridgewell)

Decision quality

3–5 candidates. Each evaluated against the actual role challenge. A clear recommendation with reasoning. You make a better decision with less noise.

Volume Recruitment

Speed and output

15–20 CVs. You do the filtering. No view on who fits and why. Speed is the SLA. The decision is left to you, without the context to make it well.

How We Assess

We test judgement.
Not interview performance.

A candidate who interviews well and a candidate who will perform are often different people. Our evaluation is designed to tell them apart.

01

Role-specific scenarios

Candidates respond to situations drawn from the actual challenges of the role, not generic competency questions.

02

Judgment under trade-offs

We present decisions with competing constraints: speed vs quality, team vs output, short-term vs long. How they prioritise reveals more than what they know.

03

Thinking, not answers

We observe how a candidate reasons through uncertainty, not whether they arrive at the "right" answer. Polish is easy. Thinking under pressure is not.

04

Recommendation with rationale

You receive a view: who fits and why, who doesn't and why, and what to watch for in the first 90 days. Not a ranked shortlist, a decision brief.
