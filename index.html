Know before you reach out

Questions founders always ask.

Do you work only on senior roles?
We specialise in critical, senior, niche and high-consequence roles, while also supporting broader hiring when it is part of a strategic mandate.
Can you manage payroll and HR operations?
Yes. Bridgewell supports payroll coordination, contract workforce operations, employee records, HR processes and governance.
Do you help select and implement HRMS platforms?
Yes. We support selection, workflow mapping, employee-data readiness, implementation coordination and adoption governance.
What happens in the first consultation?
We clarify the business problem, test the assumptions behind the request and recommend the most sensible next step. You will not receive a generic sales presentation.
Which companies are the best fit?
Startups and mid-sized companies—typically 50 to 2,000 employees—where people decisions have become too important to remain fragmented.
